What the numbers are telling you

4 – 5

2 – 4

This is your Wheel of Life. Is it crooked or balanced? A crooked wheel struggles to move forward smoothly — how efficient can you be if you're not balanced? It's easy to become extremely satisfied in one area, but at what cost? Pour everything into career and finances, and what happens to your health, your relationships, your fun along the way? The goal isn't to trade off one area for another — it's to grow the whole wheel, pushing your boundaries so it gets bigger while staying balanced.
